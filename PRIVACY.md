# RabbitHole Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 15, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

RabbitHole is a browser extension that allows users to record browsing sessions and visualize their navigation history as interactive knowledge graphs.

RabbitHole is designed with privacy in mind. Users control when recording begins and ends, and browsing-session information is stored locally on their devices.

This Privacy Policy explains what information RabbitHole accesses, how it is used, and how users can manage it.

## 2. Information We Collect

During an active, user-initiated recording session, RabbitHole may record:

- **Page URLs:** Addresses of webpages visited during the session.
- **Page titles:** Titles of visited webpages.
- **Timestamps:** When navigation events occur.
- **Navigation relationships:** Connections between visited webpages.
- **Session information:** User-created session names and associated browsing events.

RabbitHole also stores user preferences, including visualization and appearance settings.

URLs and page titles may sometimes contain personal or sensitive information, depending on the websites visited.

## 3. When Information Is Recorded

RabbitHole records navigation information only when a user explicitly starts a recording session.

Users can stop recording through the extension interface.

RabbitHole does not intentionally record browsing navigation outside active sessions.

## 4. How Information Is Used

Recorded information is used exclusively to provide RabbitHole's core functionality, including:

- Generating interactive knowledge graphs of browsing sessions.
- Displaying connections between visited webpages.
- Allowing users to revisit previously explored webpages.
- Saving and managing research sessions.
- Supporting session export and import.

RabbitHole does not use recorded information for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness assessments, or unrelated purposes.

## 5. Data Storage and Sharing

RabbitHole stores browsing-session information locally using the browser's extension storage system, such as `chrome.storage.local`.

RabbitHole does not require users to create accounts.

RabbitHole does not transmit recorded browsing-session data to developer-operated servers.

RabbitHole does not sell or share recorded browsing-session data with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

Users may choose to export session information to a JSON file. Exported files are managed by the user and may contain recorded browsing history.

## 6. Browser Permissions

RabbitHole requests the following browser permissions:

**Storage:** Used to save recorded sessions, navigation events, and user preferences locally.

**Tabs:** Used to observe webpage navigation during active recording sessions, retrieve page information, open the interactive graph explorer, and revisit recorded webpages.

These permissions are used to support RabbitHole's primary purpose of visualizing browsing research sessions.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Recorded sessions remain in local browser storage until the user deletes them or the browser removes the extension's stored data.

Users can delete saved sessions through RabbitHole's interface.

Users can also remove the extension's locally stored data through their browser's extension management and storage controls.

RabbitHole does not maintain a separate server-side copy of recorded sessions.

## 8. Data Security

RabbitHole is designed to minimize unnecessary data collection and transmission.

Recorded session data remains in the browser's local extension storage rather than being uploaded to a remote database.

Users should be aware that exported JSON files may contain sensitive browsing information and should be stored or shared carefully.

## 9. Children's Privacy

RabbitHole is not specifically designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's personal information through developer-operated services.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as RabbitHole's functionality changes.

Updates will be published on this page, along with a revised "Last Updated" date when appropriate.
